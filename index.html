THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Rooted in curiosity.
Moving between worlds.

I'm Jasper, a German living and creating in Argentina. My path weaves together software, regenerative land projects, music, and spaces for human connection.

I find meaning in translating between disciplines: an idea becomes a usable tool; a landscape becomes a place to learn; a conversation becomes an invitation to collaborate.

I'm less interested in choosing one professional label than in building with care, curiosity, and the people around me.
